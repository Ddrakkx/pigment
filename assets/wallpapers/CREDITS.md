Steam Workshop recommendations link to their original pages. Their files and previews are not redistributed with Pigment. Online previews are fetched into the local cache when browsing that optional selection.

The two bundled live wallpapers, Pigment Aurora (Сияние) and Pigment Orbit (Орбита), are original procedural artwork: SVG previews and canvas animations, distributed under the project's MIT license. They use no remote resources, audio or third-party pictures. Their complete source and license are in assets/live/pigment-aurora and assets/live/pigment-orbit.
